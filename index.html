About

I study how the brain works through the lens of its physical substrate. Working with Nicolas Brunel (Bocconi U, Milan) and Nicho Hatsopoulos (U Chicago), I use neurobiological insight, machine learning frameworks, and physics methods to understand the distinct operating modes of complex neural systems as they carry out their many functions—all under the constraints of finite space and time. Currently I am studying the motor system. Previously I studied working memory with Xiao-Jing Wang (NYU), and vision with Lyle Muller (Western U, near Toronto) in collaboration with John Reynolds (Salk Institute, San Diego).
