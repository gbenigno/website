About

I study how the brain works through the lens of its physical substrate. Working with Nicolas Brunel (Bocconi University, Milan) and Nicho Hatsopoulos (University of Chicago), I use neurobiological insight, machine learning frameworks, and physics methods to understand operating modes of complex neural systems as they carry out their functions—under the constraints of finite space and time. Currently I focus on the motor system.

Previously: working memory with Xiao‑Jing Wang (NYU); vision with Lyle Muller (Western University) in collaboration with John Reynolds (Salk Institute).
